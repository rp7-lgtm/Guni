Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GUNI uses the information you share with us to respond to shirt inquiries and handle order requests. This policy explains what information is involved, how it is used, and how you can contact us about it. It applies to this website and to correspondence you send through its inquiry and order functions.

Information we collect

When you contact GUNI or make an order inquiry, we receive the information you choose to send, including your name, email address, message, and selected shirt details. These details may include the design, size, and quantity you want to order. Keep your message relevant to the inquiry and leave out passwords, identification documents, and payment-card information.

How we use your information

We use your information to answer questions about our shirts, clarify order details, and communicate with you about your request. Information you provide for an inquiry or order is used for that purpose. Any use beyond handling your request requires a separate explanation and, where required, your permission.

Contact and order emails

Our inquiry and order forms prepare a message in your email application. You can review the recipient, subject, and contents before sending it. GUNI receives the message when you send it through your email service; preparing a draft alone does not place or confirm an order. Further correspondence may be needed to confirm the selected items and other order arrangements.

Sharing your information

GUNI does not sell customer information or share inquiry details for unrelated advertising. Access to correspondence is limited to those handling your request. Email and website service providers process information as part of delivering their services, under their own privacy policies. If completing your request requires sharing additional information with another party, we will explain what is needed before proceeding, except where disclosure is required by law.

Website services

This website uses Bootstrap resources delivered through jsDelivr and typography supplied by Google Fonts. Loading these resources connects your browser to those services, which may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address. Their handling of that information is governed by their respective privacy policies. External websites linked from GUNI also operate under their own policies.

Keeping and protecting correspondence

We retain inquiry and order correspondence for as long as it is needed to handle the request, resolve related concerns, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Access is restricted to people who need the information for those purposes. We take reasonable care in handling correspondence, while recognizing that email and internet transmission carry security risks.

Your choices and requests

You can browse our shirt selections without sending an inquiry. Before sending an email, you can change the information in the draft or discard it. If you have already contacted us, you may ask about the information you provided, request a correction, or request its deletion by writing to hello@examplewebsite142.com. We will review your request and explain any information we need to retain to address an ongoing order, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when our website or information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. For questions about this policy or the information you have shared with GUNI, contact hello@examplewebsite142.com. Send shirt-order inquiries to orders@examplewebsite142.com.
