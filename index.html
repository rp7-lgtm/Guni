About us

The brand name “GUNI” – inspired by the initial idea to incorporate the Filipino touch – is derived from the Tagalog term “guni-guni,” which translates to imagination. We aim to sell functional T-Shirt designs with a retro, minimalistic twist, and hope to embody a straightforward approach in building a shopping experience for our customers.
